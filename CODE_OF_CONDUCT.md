# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This repository is for respectful, technically rigorous collaboration.

We expect contributors to:

- communicate clearly and in good faith
- focus feedback on code, design, behavior, and facts
- assume mistakes are fixable and discuss them constructively
- respect different levels of experience and different backgrounds
- keep security, privacy, and operational safety in mind when proposing changes

## Unacceptable behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable in this project:

- harassment, personal attacks, threats, or intimidation
- discriminatory language or conduct
- bad-faith arguments, trolling, or deliberately inflammatory behavior
- sharing private, sensitive, or security-relevant information without authorization
- repeated disruption of reviews, issues, or discussions after clear maintainer direction

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in:

- issues, pull requests, commits, and review discussions in this repository
- project-related chat, design reviews, and incident follow-up tied to this repository

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, contact the repository maintainers privately.

When reporting, include:

- what happened
- where it happened
- links, screenshots, or other relevant context
- whether there is any immediate safety or confidentiality concern

## Maintainer responsibilities

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, or pull requests that do not align with this Code of Conduct.

They may also take any appropriate action, including:

- private warning
- temporary participation limits
- removal from project spaces
- permanent ban from project participation

## Enforcement

Maintainers will review reports promptly and handle them as confidentially as reasonably possible.

Enforcement decisions are based on protecting contributors, the project, and the integrity of technical collaboration.
